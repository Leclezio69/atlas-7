FIG. 02 / SIGNAL & NOISE

The hard part is the middle band. A breach is easy to call. A watch-band reading could be a mild defect or noise, and the game will not tell you which.

Within tolerance

Clear it and move on, or spend the hours elsewhere.

Watch band

Raise it, clear it, or pay one to three hours for a follow-up that settles it.

Breach

Raise it. The question is how severe, and whether you hold that line on Friday.

False alarms cost you. Every finding that turns out to be noise loses precision points and credibility with the committee.

06 · The rules

The disciplines behind
the decisions.

The scenario uses model-risk, fair-lending and AI-governance themes. References provide training context; the simulation is not an authoritative statement of current requirements.

Federal Reserve / OCCSR 11-7 · OCC 2011-12

Conceptual soundness, outcomes analysis, ongoing monitoring, implementation and vendor products. The spine of the test plan.

CFPBECOA · Regulation B §1002.9

Principal reasons on adverse action notices. Why unstable reason codes are a High, not a footnote.

Fair lending practiceFour-fifths screen · BISG

Adverse impact ratios, proxy estimation and the search for a less discriminatory alternative.

European UnionAI Act · Annex III 5(b)

Creditworthiness scoring as high-risk AI, with Articles 10, 11, 15 and 86 on data, documentation, accuracy and explanation.

Interagency, 2023Third-party risk guidance

Why a bundled embedding with no development evidence is your problem, not the vendor's.
